# Descenders reporting

Accepted product direction, 2026-09-07. Implementation status is separate in [delivery status](docs/delivery/status.md).

Help a coach tell a useful, truthful story to an athlete about growth, consistency and participation. Published race results are the evidence. Starts and personal progress deserve attention alongside placing and podiums. MILO quietly guides the voice: Make the effort, Include everyone, Learn by trying, Offer encouragement.

The application began as an engineering exploration of league results. The race playground developed the design-led coaching experience. For experiential judgment, the accepted playground direction and the owner's current instructions take precedence over earlier engineering-led layouts. Financial Times-style storytelling means a meaningful observation, a chart chosen to explain it, direct labels and context. It is not a palette or a license to infer what the records cannot establish.

The landing page synthesizes the current season and offers the member's personal squad. From there, a coach can explore another squad, a race review, an athlete's season and the field they raced in. Tables remain available as evidence. They do not dictate every page's composition.

This is becoming a real application: durable squad definitions and membership, invitations, administration, authenticated local PGlite operation and deployment readiness are in scope. It remains a reporting product rather than a general practice, messaging or volunteer-management platform.

Read [editorial direction](docs/design/editorial-direction.md) for accepted visual decisions, [ADR-0005](docs/adr/0005-persistent-club-administration.md) for persistent writes, and the [delivery plan](docs/delivery/plan.md) for acceptance evidence. The domain glossary, published source semantics and privacy rules continue to constrain every claim and data flow.
